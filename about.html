About Guwahati Rent

Guwahati Rent is a free, anonymous map of what people in Guwahati actually pay in rent. Brokers and listing sites show asking prices, which are often higher than what tenants really pay. Here, renters pin their own rent, so the next person flat-hunting can see real numbers for each area.

It is for anyone renting, or about to rent, in Guwahati: students looking for a PG or a room, people moving to the city, and families comparing areas.

How it works

Tap Pin your rent, move the map until the pin is on your home (on a computer you can also click the spot), and fill in a short form. No login, no name, no phone number. Locations are rounded to about 100 metres. Flats, houses, 1RKs, rooms and PGs can all be pinned.

Only the basics about the home and the rent are needed. The form also asks, optionally, about water supply, power backup and power cuts, and whether water collects after heavy rain, because in Guwahati these matter as much as the rent. Turn on Monsoon waterlogging on the map to see where renters reported it.

Prefer a table? Choose List above the map to see the same rents as a sortable list.

To-Let boards

Spotted a To-Let board? Tap Spot a To-Let, take a photo, and it goes on the map once we have checked it. The phone number stays on the board itself; we never type it in or read it out. Boards are removed after 60 days, or sooner if people report them gone.

Can I trust the numbers?

Every rent is self-reported and unverified. We reject rents outside sensible ranges, limit how many pins one device can add, and let the community report fake ones. Treat the map as a guide, and always see a flat in person before paying anything.

Rents can also be out of date. The map hides rents older than 18 months by default, and the medians under Stats use only the last 18 months and only groups with at least 3 rents. Water, power and monsoon details describe one home as its renter saw it, not the whole area, and are not official data.
