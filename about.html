About Guwahati Rent

Guwahati Rent is a free, anonymous map of what people in Guwahati actually pay in rent. Brokers and listing sites show asking prices, which are often higher than what tenants really pay. Here, renters pin their own rent, so the next person flat-hunting can see real numbers for each area.

How it works

Tap Pin your rent, tap roughly where your home is, and fill in a short form. No login, no name, no phone number. Locations are rounded to about 100 metres.

We also ask whether your area waterlogs in the monsoon, because in Guwahati that matters as much as the rent.

To-Let boards

Spotted a To-Let board? Tap Spot a To-Let, take a photo, and it goes on the map once we have checked it. The phone number stays on the board itself; we never type it in or read it out. Boards are removed after 60 days, or sooner if people report them gone.

Coming next

Owners listing flats directly (no broker) and flat-seekers getting matched by email.

Can I trust the numbers?

Every rent is self-reported and unverified. We reject rents outside sensible ranges, limit how many pins one device can add, and let the community report fake ones. Treat the map as a guide, and always see a flat in person before paying anything.
