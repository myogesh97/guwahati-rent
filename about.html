About Guwahati Rent

Guwahati Rent is a free, anonymous map of what people in Guwahati actually pay in rent. Brokers and listing sites show asking prices, which are often higher than what tenants really pay. Here, renters pin their own rent, so the next person flat-hunting can see real numbers for each area.

It is for anyone renting, or about to rent, in Guwahati: students looking for a PG or a room, people moving to the city, and families comparing areas.

How it works

Tap Pin your rent, move the map until the pin is on your home (on a computer you can also click the spot), and fill in a short form. No login, no name, no phone number. Locations are rounded to about %POLICY.pinRoundingM% metres. Flats, houses, 1RKs, rooms and PGs can all be pinned.

Only the basics about the home and the rent are needed. The form also asks, optionally, about water supply, power backup and power cuts, and whether water collects after heavy rain, because in Guwahati these matter as much as the rent. Turn on Monsoon waterlogging on the map to see where renters reported it.

Prefer a table? Choose List above the map to see the same rents as a sortable list.

To-Let boards

Spotted a To-Let board? Tap Spot a To-Let, take a photo, and it goes on the map once we have checked it. The phone number stays on the board itself; we never type it in or read it out. Boards are removed after %POLICY.boardExpiryDays% days, or sooner if people report them gone.

Can I trust the numbers?

Every rent is self-reported and unverified. We reject rents outside sensible ranges, limit how many pins one device can add, and let the community report fake ones. Treat the map as a guide, and always see a flat in person before paying anything.

Rents can also be out of date. The map hides rents older than %POLICY.statsWindowMonths% months by default, and the medians under Stats use only the last %POLICY.statsWindowMonths% months and only groups with at least %POLICY.minSample% rents. Water, power and monsoon details describe one home as its renter saw it, not the whole area, and are not official data.

Questions

How does Guwahati Rent work?

Renters pin the rent they pay on a map of Guwahati and answer a short form about the home. No login, no name, no phone number. Locations are rounded to about %POLICY.pinRoundingM% metres. Under Stats you see median rents for each area.

Is it anonymous?

Yes. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and locations are rounded to about %POLICY.pinRoundingM% metres. The building or society name you type is shown only once at least %POLICY.societyMinPins% rents within %POLICY.societyRadiusM% metres give the same name. To stop abuse we store a salted hash of your IP address and of a random ID kept in your browser. A hash cannot be turned back into your IP.

Is it free?

Yes. Pinning a rent and looking at the map are free. We do not sell data, show ads, or work with brokers.

How are unusual rents handled?

Rents that are far from others of the same size nearby are marked as unusual and not counted in medians. Medians use only rents from the last %POLICY.statsWindowMonths% months, and only groups with at least %POLICY.minSample% rents. A pin reported by %POLICY.reportThreshold% people is hidden from the map until we review it.

How do I delete my pin?

From the same browser you used to add it: tap the pin and choose Delete my pin. If you cleared your browser data, contact us and we will remove it.

Who runs Guwahati Rent?

It is run independently of brokers and listing sites. We do not sell data, show ads, or work with brokers. Contact details are on the Privacy page.
