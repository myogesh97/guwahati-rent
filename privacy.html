Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Plain-language summary of what this site collects today. This policy will be updated before listing and matching features launch.

What we collect when you pin your rent

  • Public: the rent, type of home, bedrooms, furnishing, whether it is gated, parking, the optional details you fill in (building name, floor, water supply and cost, monsoon waterlogging, power, deposit, maintenance, size, the date you last saw these conditions, and short notes), and an approximate location rounded to about 100 metres. These are shown as reported by a renter, not verified by us.
  • Not collected: your name, email, phone number or exact address. Please do not write personal details in the free-text fields.

Spam prevention

To stop abuse we store a salted hash of your IP address and of a random ID kept in your browser. A hash cannot be turned back into your IP. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a real person is submitting. Cloudflare processes your request for that check under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your pin

When you add a pin, your browser keeps a secret code that lets you delete it later from the same browser: tap the pin and choose Delete my pin. If you cleared your browser data, contact us (see below) and we will remove it.

Services we use

Supabase (database), Cloudflare (spam check), Netlify (hosting), OpenStreetMap and Protomaps (map data). We do not sell data, show ads, or work with brokers.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions: a contact email will be added here before public launch.
