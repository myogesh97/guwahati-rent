Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Plain-language summary of what this site collects today. This policy will be updated before listing and matching features launch.

What we collect when you pin your rent

  • Public: the rent, type of home, bedrooms, furnishing, whether it is gated, parking, the optional details you fill in (floor, water supply and cost, monsoon waterlogging, power, deposit, maintenance, the date you last saw these conditions, and short notes), and an approximate location rounded to about %POLICY.pinRoundingM% metres. These are shown as reported by a renter, not verified by us.
  • Shown only in a group: the building or society name, if you type one, appears on the map only once at least %POLICY.societyMinPins% rents within %POLICY.societyRadiusM% metres give the same name. On its own, together with the other details, it could point to one flat. Flat and house numbers are not accepted.
  • Shown as a range: the size of the home (for example 500–800 sq ft), never the exact number.
  • Not collected: your name, email, phone number or exact address. Please do not write personal details in the free-text fields.

To-Let board photos

  • What we keep: a smaller copy of your photo, made in your browser, and the board's location to about %POLICY.boardRoundingM% metres (a board is public signage, so it needs to be findable). The copy is re-drawn from the pixels, so the camera's hidden details (including GPS position, phone model and time) are never uploaded.
  • Checked first: every photo is reviewed by us before anyone else can see it. Photos showing people's faces, number plates or private details are rejected and deleted.
  • Deleted automatically: rejected photos straight away, and every board after %POLICY.boardExpiryDays% days. If %POLICY.reportThreshold% people report a board gone, it is hidden from the map at once and we review it; removed boards and their photos are deleted within a day.
  • Not collected: your name, email or phone number. We never read or store the phone number written on the board; it stays in the photo.

Reporting a pin

When you report a pin we keep the reason and your optional note. For every report (pins and "board is gone") we also keep salted hashes of a random ID from your browser and of your IP address, so one person counts once. A pin reported by %POLICY.reportThreshold% people is hidden from the map until we review it. The note is seen only by our moderators, never shown publicly, and deleted %POLICY.reportNoteRetentionDays% days after the report is resolved. Please don't put names or phone numbers in it.

Spam prevention

To stop abuse we store a salted hash of your IP address and of a random ID kept in your browser. A hash cannot be turned back into your IP. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a real person is submitting. Cloudflare processes your request for that check under its own privacy policy.

Your consent

Every form (adding a rent, a To-Let photo, or a report) has a box you tick to agree to this policy and the Terms before you send it. It is never ticked for you, and we do not treat browsing the map as agreement. You can withdraw by deleting your pin (below).

Deleting your pin

When you add a pin, your browser keeps a secret code that lets you delete it later from the same browser: tap the pin and choose Delete my pin. If you cleared your browser data, contact us (see below) and we will remove it.

Services we use

Supabase (database), Cloudflare (spam check), Netlify (hosting), OpenStreetMap and Protomaps (map data). We do not sell data, show ads, or work with brokers.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions: a contact email will be added here before public launch.
